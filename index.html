Guides for online sellers

Short, practical explainers on the numbers behind your listings.

How to calculate true profit per sale

Add up every variable cost — product, packaging, shipping, ads, and marketplace fees — then subtract it from the selling price. Percentage-based fees are the most commonly forgotten cost.

The profit calculator does this in one step and also shows your break-even price.

Why margin and markup are not the same

Margin is profit divided by selling price. Markup is profit divided by cost. A 50% markup is only a 33.3% margin — mixing them up is one of the most expensive pricing mistakes sellers make.

When a discount still leaves a profit

A discount eats directly into margin. If your margin is 20% and you offer a 15% discount, you may drop below break-even once fees are added back. Run the numbers with the discount calculator before launching a sale.
