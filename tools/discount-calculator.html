How to use the discount calculator

  1. Enter the original listed price before any discount.
  2. Enter the discount percentage (0 to 100).
  3. Read the discount amount, final sale price, and total savings instantly.

Formula

Discount amount = original price × discount % ÷ 100
Final sale price = original price − discount amount
Savings = discount amount

A shirt is listed at ₹1,200 with a 25% discount.

Frequently asked questions

Can the discount be over 100%?

No. This calculator accepts values from 0% to 100%. A 100% discount makes the final price zero.

Does it work with any currency?

Yes. The math is currency-agnostic; results are displayed in ₹. Read the numbers as your local currency if you prefer.

What about stacked discounts?

Apply them one at a time. If a product has 20% off and then an extra 10%, run the result of the first discount as the original price of the second.

Does it include tax?

No. Enter the pre-tax price to see the discount before tax. Add tax separately if needed.
