About GoSoping

GoSoping is a free library of online business tools built for e-commerce sellers, small business owners, entrepreneurs, and everyday shoppers. Our goal is simple: give people accurate, honest tools that answer real business questions without a spreadsheet, a sign-up form, or a subscription.

Why we built it

Most small sellers run on spreadsheets and guesswork. Marketplace fees, ad spend, shipping, and packaging are easy to lose track of — and one forgotten cost can turn a "profitable" product into a loss on every sale. GoSoping puts the important math in one place, with clear formulas so you understand what's happening.

How it works

Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and nothing is stored. You don't need an account, and we don't collect personal information to use the tools.

What we're not

GoSoping is educational. We don't provide tax, legal, or financial advice, and we don't guarantee specific business results or advertising outcomes. For anything that affects your taxes or legal obligations, consult a qualified professional.
