Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

This policy explains what data GoSoping collects (very little) and how it's used.

Data you enter into the tools

All tool inputs are processed entirely in your browser. They are never transmitted to our servers and are not stored anywhere. When you close or refresh the page, the values are gone.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited and which tools are used most. If we do, we configure it to collect aggregated, non-identifying information and we do not build profiles of individual visitors.

Cookies

GoSoping itself does not require cookies for the tools to work. If we add analytics or advertising, those third parties may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

Advertising

If GoSoping displays advertising in the future, ads may be served by third-party networks (such as Google AdSense) which may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

Children's privacy

GoSoping is intended for a general business audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date will change as well.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@gosoping.com.
